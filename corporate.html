Why the numbers drop at each stage

Offers decline at pre-screen for value or category, and again at inspection for condition or authenticity. Five accepted lots did not meet their reserve and were returned to their donors under the agreed terms.

Reporting a drive by items offered rather than by net proceeds would overstate it by a factor of two.

What a report contains

  • Contributions received, by programme and by date, with settlement references.
  • Allocations made against each restriction, and any allocation refused because it breached one.
  • Disbursements to schools, with payment references and reconciliation dates.
  • Outcomes the schools confirmed, and the cases where they did not.
  • Refunds, chargebacks and reallocations, shown rather than netted off quietly.

Published quarterly in the workspace. The annual figures are prepared alongside the charity's statutory accounts.
