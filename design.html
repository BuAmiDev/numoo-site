Home/Design system

One loop, carried everywhere.

The wordmark already holds the system: two loops that close. The interface is photo-led, set in a round geometric sans that shares the logo’s drawing, and lit by the blue-to-cyan light of the mark.

The mark

Wordmark

Used at a single weight, tinted from the current text colour rather than shipped in two files. It inherits the theme, so there is no wrong version on a dark background.

mask-image, background-color: currentColor
The loop alone

Carries meaning rather than ornament: it marks the points where the two circuits are being described. It is never used as a generic bullet in body text.

.inf, .inf--lg
Case reference marks

Generated from the case ID, so every case is recognisable without a photograph. Deterministic, so the same case always looks the same.

NUMOO.sigil(id)

Colour

Two hues from the logo carry the two circuits. Green, amber and red are reserved for state and are never used to tell one series from another. Neutrals are biased towards the brand blue rather than being pure greys.

Chart palette

Four categorical hues, validated for colour-vision deficiency separation and surface contrast in both themes before use. Two of the light-mode hues sit below 3:1 against white, so every chart that uses them also carries direct labels and a table view.

worst adjacent pair ΔE 15.1 deutan, light · 13.2 deutan, dark

Dark theme

Chosen, not inverted. Surfaces move to a navy rather than to grey, the brand blue lightens to hold contrast, and the chart hues are re-stepped and re-validated against the darker surface.

Type

Outfit display · headings, numbers · 500–700, tracking −3.5%

A round geometric sans with the same circular bowls as the numoo wordmark. Set large and tight, it gives the confident, modern voice of the reference sites while staying a relative of the logo.

DM Sans interface and body · 400–700

Quiet and highly legible at small sizes, so forms, tables and long explanations stay calm beneath the bold headlines.

AED 18,000.00 قراءة عربية · Readex Pro money · Outfit with tabular figures

Every amount, reference, timestamp and countdown. Money that cannot be compared down a column is money a donor cannot check.

Scale

Fluid between 400px and 1280px. Body copy is held under 66 characters. Arabic switches to Readex Pro, drawn to sit with Outfit.

Components

A control names what happens. Gold belongs to the auction circuit only. A disabled control keeps its reason in a title, never in silence.

Money trail

The signature component. It appears on a case, on an item, on an order and on an application, because the same question is being answered each time: where is it now, and who moved it?

Bilingual and bidirectional

Every rule is written with logical properties rather than left and right, so switching to Arabic flips the layout rather than patching it. The language control in the header is live on every page.

  • margin-inline, padding-inline, inset-inline-start throughout. No directional margins anywhere in the stylesheet.
  • Gradients, drawer transforms and select arrows are mirrored explicitly under [dir="rtl"].
  • Numerals stay in Western Arabic figures for money, matching UAE banking and school invoices.
  • Arabic type selection, line height and the translation itself are a separate pass with a native writer, not a machine translation.

Accessibility

  • Body text meets 4.5:1 and large text 3:1 in both themes. Muted greys were darkened until they did.
  • Every interactive element has a visible focus ring in the brand cyan, including chart bars, which are focusable buttons with amount labels.
  • Colour never carries meaning alone: state chips have words, chart series have direct labels and a table view.
  • Motion is limited to one drawn line on the diagram and the meters filling. Both stop under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Targets are at least 40px tall. The layout reflows to one column at 600px with no horizontal scroll at 320px.
  • Countdowns update once a second in text, which is not announced continuously to assistive technology.

What the system deliberately does not have

Photographs of people

No component takes a beneficiary image, so no future screen can quietly introduce one.

A single card style

Border, fill and shadow are applied by role. Four radii exist and each one means something different.

Urgency theatre

No red counters on cases, no scarcity language. Only auction lots have a timer, because only an auction actually closes.
