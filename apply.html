What you’ll need
  • Emirates ID of the parent or guardian
  • The school’s fee invoice or arrears statement
  • Proof of income, or a letter explaining its loss
  • Tenancy contract or proof of address
Photos of documents are fine. Medical records and bank statements are not requested.

What happens after you apply

Most families hear back within 14 days. One case officer looks after you from start to finish.

Will anyone know my family applied?

No. Your application is seen only by the charity’s case officers and committee. The school confirms fees but does not see your documents.

Do I receive the money?

No. Approved support is paid directly to the school against its invoice. You never need to handle or account for a payment.

What if I don’t have every document?

Submit what you have. Your case officer will tell you exactly what is still needed, and your application waits until it arrives.

Do I have to agree to a public campaign?

No. Many cases are paid from the Education Fund with no public page. Saying no never affects the decision.

Will I be asked to give back later?

Only if you want to. Any future pledge to give back is voluntary and never a condition of support.
