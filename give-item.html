What we can accept

The pilot starts with categories an independent specialist can assess reliably, valued from AED 1,000.

Accepted
  • Handbags and leather goods
  • Watches, mechanical and quartz
  • Jewellery, with documentation
  • Pens, cameras and collectables
  • Studio ceramics and signed works
Not accepted
  • Restricted, hazardous or licensed goods
  • Items that are shared, disputed or financed
  • Counterfeits, or items no specialist can assess
  • Electronics holding personal data
  • Anything that can’t be stored or insured safely
Not sure if it qualifies? Send a photo with your offer and a case officer will tell you within two working days. Ask about an item

Offer an item

Tell us what it is. A case officer reviews your offer within two working days and either books a collection or explains why it can’t be accepted.

1. Describe itPhotos, condition and your estimate
2. We collect itFrom your door, with a signed custody receipt
3. A specialist assesses itMethod, result and limits all published
4. It sells at auctionSeven days, with a reserve you agree
5. You see what it raisedNet proceeds, and the case or fund they reached
The item stays yours until you sign the custody receipt at collection. You can withdraw the offer any time before that.
