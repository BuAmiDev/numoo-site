What a bid commits you to

1A bid is an offer to pay

Bidding is open to registered adults with a verified contact. If you win, you owe the amount. There is no cooling-off period written into the sale.

2The reserve may not be met

Every lot carries a reserve set with the donor. If bidding closes below it, the lot does not sell and the donor's agreed terms decide what happens next.

3Condition is described, not hidden

Faults, wear and missing accessories are listed in the lot report. The specialist's limitations are published alongside their conclusion.

4Delivery is charged separately

A delivery charge is added at checkout and matched against the courier cost. It is never counted as charitable proceeds.

Donors, the staff who handled a lot, and anyone with a declared conflict cannot bid on it. Bids are recorded with server time and cannot be withdrawn.
