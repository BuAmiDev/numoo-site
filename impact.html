How these numbers are counted

Reporting rules decided before the first campaign, so that a good quarter and a bad one are measured the same way.

An allocation is not income

Moving money from a pooled fund to a case is reported as an allocation. It never appears again as a new donation.

Pledges are not cash

A corporate commitment is reported as pledged until it is received. The two figures are never combined.

Gross is not net

A lot that sold for AED 2,200 did not deliver AED 2,200 to a case. Sale costs are disclosed and deducted before the figure is reported.

A payment is not an outcome

Paying a school proves the money moved. Continuity is a separate measure, confirmed later by the school.

Failures are counted too

Unconfirmed continuity, unsold lots, refunds and declined cases all appear. A report with no bad numbers is not a report.

No child is a case study

No photographs, names or grades are collected for marketing. Stories are never used to raise more than the verified need.
