The controls are the product.

Anyone can build a page that collects money. The part worth building carefully is everything between a card being charged and a school confirming a child is still in class.

Where money sits, and what it is called there

Collection Received

Cleared payments only. Pending authorisations are shown apart from cleared receipts so a target cannot look met when it is not.

Ledger Restricted

Each receipt carries its restriction: a case, a fund, a corporate programme. The link from source to use survives every later movement.

Approval Allocated

An allocation moves money against a case. It is not a new donation and is never counted as income twice.

Settlement Disbursed

Paid out to a verified third-party account, matched to a bank statement line, with the reference visible to the donors who funded it.

What happens when things go wrong

Exception handling
SituationWhat the platform does
A payment is attempted twiceIdempotency keys stop a duplicate receipt or a duplicate ledger posting. One payment, one record.
A case overfunds in the same momentCollection pauses at the target. Excess is refunded, or moved only with the donor's explicit agreement.
A case expires underfundedIt returns to the committee for a top-up from the fund, a reduced scope agreed with the school, or a refund. Donors are told which.
A family withdrawsThe campaign closes, the public page is removed, and donors choose a refund or reallocation.
A winning bidder does not payReminders, then cancellation and account controls. The next bidder may be offered the lot only on a fresh, explicit acceptance.
A lot fails authenticationIt is never listed. The agreed lawful return or dispute procedure applies. Nothing is destroyed or disposed of without the donor's agreed terms.
An item is lost or damaged in custodyA custody investigation runs against the handover records, and the agreed liability and insurance process follows.
A chargeback arrivesThe linked allocation is reversed in the journal. A disbursed payment is reported as a loss, never silently absorbed by another case.

Privacy and dignity

  • Public case pages are generated from redacted fields. The application record itself is never the source of a public page.
  • Names, faces, school names and precise class details are not published, and a reference mark is generated from the case ID rather than from anything personal.
  • Evidence is encrypted, retained on a schedule by category, and access to it is logged by person and by record.
  • Case officers see the file. Logistics staff see a collection address. Specialists see an item. Corporate staff see aggregates.
  • Correction and deletion requests are honoured within the charity's legal retention duties.
  • Identity documents, medical details and children's records never appear in marketing material, screenshots or demonstrations.

What is still open

An honest proposal names the things it has not settled. These need the charity's decision or an authority's confirmation before launch.

  • OpenFundraising permissions for each campaign and for charity auctions specifically.
  • OpenZakat: excluded from the first release until segregation and specialist governance exist.
  • OpenConsumer protection and return rights as they apply to charity auction sales.
  • OpenTax treatment of buyer charges, and identity checks above a threshold.
  • OpenThe direct-support authority limit, below which no public campaign is used.
  • OpenWhether operating costs are deducted from proceeds or met by a sponsor.

The UAE requires fundraising to run through a licensed and authorised body. numoo is operated by such a charity; the technology provider neither collects nor holds charitable funds.

What is proved before launch

Acceptance is not a feature list. These are the behaviours that are demonstrated against the real system before a single public campaign opens.
