Why there is no photograph

A child who needed help should not be recognisable years later because of it. Cases carry a reference number and a generated mark, never a face, a name or a class.

Why the amounts are odd

The target is the school invoice minus any discount the school agreed and any scholarship already in place. It is what remains, not what was asked for.

What happens if a case overfunds

Collection pauses at the target. Anything received in the same moment is refunded or, with the donor's explicit agreement, moved to the Education Fund.
