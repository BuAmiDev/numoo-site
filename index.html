Does any money go to the family directly?

No. Every payment goes to the school or examination board, against an invoice the charity has verified, to a bank account it confirmed independently.

How do I know a case is real?

Income, tenancy, guardianship and the school invoice are checked by two case officers, the school confirms the balance directly, and a committee records its decision before anything is published.

What happens if a case doesn’t reach its target?

It goes back to the committee, which can top it up from the Education Fund, agree a smaller scope with the school, or refund donors. You are told which.

Can I donate in honour or memory of someone?

Yes. Add their name at checkout and it appears on your receipt. You can also give anonymously.

Is my donation Zakat eligible?

Not in the first release. Zakat needs its own segregated fund and governance, and the charity will open that route separately.

Can I set up a monthly donation?

Yes, to the Education Fund or to cases you choose. Pause, change or stop it any time from My numoo.
