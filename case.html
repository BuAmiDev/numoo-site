Where your money will go

Questions donors ask

Can the family spend this on something else?
No. Funds are paid to the school's verified account against the invoice. The family never receives a transfer.
What if the case does not reach its target?
It returns to the committee, which may top it up from the Education Fund, reduce the scope with the school, or refund donors. Donors are told which.
Is this Zakat eligible?
Not at this stage. Zakat requires segregated accounting and its own governance, and the charity has not opened that route yet. Ordinary donations are never labelled Zakat eligible by default.
Will I be told what happened?
You receive the payment confirmation and a continuity check at the end of the term. You will not receive grades, photographs or documents.
